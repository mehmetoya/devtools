About this tool

QR codes store text, links or structured payloads (Wi-Fi credentials, contact cards, mail and SMS links) in a square matrix. The version (grid size) is picked automatically from the amount of data. A higher error-correction level lets a damaged or partly covered code still scan, at the cost of a denser grid. Text is encoded as UTF-8.

EAN-13 and EAN-8 are the retail barcodes printed on most products. Enter 12 (or 7) digits and the final check digit is calculated for you; enter all 13 (or 8) and the check digit is verified. Each digit is drawn with one of three bar patterns — L (odd parity), G (even parity) or R — and for EAN-13 the mix of L and G patterns on the left half encodes the leading digit.
