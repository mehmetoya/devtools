About this tool

The converter first breaks your text into words. Spaces, hyphens, underscores, dots and other punctuation count as separators, and so do lower-to-upper transitions inside identifiers. Runs of capitals are treated as acronyms, so parseHTTPResponse becomes the words parse, HTTP and Response.

  • UPPER / lower / tOGGLE change letters in place and keep everything else untouched.
  • Title capitalises the first letter of every word; Sentence capitalises only the start of each sentence.
  • camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case, CONSTANT_CASE rebuild the text from its words, dropping punctuation.

With Convert each line separately turned on, every line is converted on its own — handy for a list of field names.

Letter rules picks the language used for upper/lower case. Choose Turkish or Azerbaijani for dotted and dotless i: ÇAĞRI ÖZTÜRK becomes çağrıÖztürk in camelCase, not çağriÖztürk. The default follows your browser's language. Open All conversions to see every style at once.
