About this tool

  • Whole text — mirrors the entire input, so the last character comes first.
  • Line order — keeps every line as-is but lists them bottom to top.
  • Word order — reverses the sequence of words on each line while leaving the spacing in place.
  • Each line — mirrors the characters of every line but keeps the lines in their original order.
  • Each word — mirrors the letters inside every word without moving the words.

Reversal works on user-perceived characters (grapheme clusters), so flags, skin-tone emoji and letters with combining accents are not torn apart. Windows line endings are normalised to \n.

Privacy

All processing happens in your browser. Nothing you type is sent, stored or logged.
