String Reverse

About this tool

  • Whole text — mirrors the entire input, so the last character comes first.
  • Line order — keeps every line as-is but lists them bottom to top.
  • Word order — reverses the sequence of words on each line while leaving the spacing in place.
  • Each line — mirrors the characters of every line but keeps the lines in their original order.
  • Each word — mirrors the letters inside every word without moving the words.

Reversal works on user-perceived characters (grapheme clusters), so flags, skin-tone emoji and letters with combining accents are not torn apart. Windows line endings are normalised to \n.

Privacy

All processing happens in your browser. Nothing you type is sent, stored or logged.

Questions

How do I reverse a string online?

Paste the text and keep Reverse set to Whole text, which mirrors the input so the last character comes first: hello becomes olleh. The result appears as you type and can be copied, downloaded or reused as input.

How do I reverse the order of lines without flipping the text?

Choose Line order. Every line stays exactly as written, but the lines are listed bottom to top. To mirror the characters inside each line while keeping the lines in place, choose Each line instead.

Will reversing text break emoji or accented letters?

No. Reversal works on user-perceived characters (grapheme clusters), so flags, skin-tone emoji and letters built from combining accents stay intact rather than being split into broken pieces.

How do I reverse the word order in a sentence?

Choose Word order. It reverses the sequence of words on each line and keeps the original spacing, so one two three becomes three two one. Each word instead mirrors the letters inside every word without moving the words.
